Bump Rush: Support

Online names are made up by the game, and there's no chat; if another player bothers you, use Mute in the player list on the results screen. For help, bugs or anything else, email bumprush@proton.me. Include your phone model and the game version, which you can find in Settings → About.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere to move. Tap DASH to slam into rivals. Each dash costs 6 energy.
  • Collect sparks to grow. Bigger creatures push harder but turn slower.
  • The arena shrinks. Stay inside the dashed ring, and be the last one standing.
  • Settings → Replay tutorial walks you through it again.

Common questions

Are the other players real? Offline matches are against bots, and every bot is labeled BOT. Online matches fill empty seats with labeled bots too.

Do I need an account? No. Your progress is saved on your device.

Is there anything to buy? No. Unlocks come from playing, and every creature plays exactly the same.

Sound or vibration is off. Check Settings: Sound, Music and Vibration each have their own switch. On iPhone, the silent switch also mutes the game.
