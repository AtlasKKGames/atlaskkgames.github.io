Bump Rush: Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Published by AtlasK

Short version: Bump Rush has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. If you play online, your nickname, look and rank badge are shared with the other players in your match while it runs, and that's all.

What stays on your device

The game saves your nickname, creature look, settings, unlocked items, rank, personal bests and match statistics in the app's local storage on your device. This data is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

Online play

When you choose to play online or join a private room, the app connects to the Bump Rush game server and sends:

  • your nickname and creature look (color, hat, eyes),
  • your rank tier (Wood to Championship), shown as a badge next to your name,
  • the type of device (iOS, Android or web), so the game can handle connection differences,
  • your game controls (joystick direction and dash presses) while you play.

The server shows your nickname, look, rank badge and in-game movements to the other players in the same match or private room. It keeps this information in memory only while the match or room exists, and does not store it or share it with anyone else. The one exception is a nickname another player reports (see below). Nicknames are filtered for offensive words before other players see them.

Reporting and muting

After an online match you can mute another player (their name shows as "Player" and their emotes are hidden, only on your device) or report their nickname. A report records the reported nickname, the time and the match number, so we can review it. It does not record who reported, or anything else about either player. Reports are kept only as long as needed to review them, and never longer than 90 days. If several players in the same match report a nickname, it is replaced with a guest name.

Like any internet service, the server's hosting provider handles your IP address to deliver network traffic. We don't use your IP address to identify you.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting services, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Children

Bump Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Offline play involves no data leaving the device at all.

Your choices

You can play entirely offline against bots. You can change your nickname at any time, hide other players' emotes in Settings, and mute or report a player after an online match. Because no personal data is stored on our servers, there's nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
