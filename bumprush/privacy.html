Bump Rush: Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Published by AtlasK

Short version: Bump Rush has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't store any information about you. Your progress stays on your device. Online, other players see a name the game makes up for you (never one you type), your creature's look and your rank badge, only while the match runs.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings, the name you type for offline play, your online name, creature look, unlocked items, rank, personal bests and match statistics in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. None of this is uploaded or backed up by us. The only exception is when you play online: your online name, look and rank tier are sent to the game server for that match, as described below. The name you type is never sent.

When the app starts

If online play is available, the app makes two small requests when it starts, even if you then only play offline, so it knows whether and where it can play online:

  • it reads a short settings file with the game server's address from our website (atlaskkgames.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages), and
  • it asks the game server whether it is running.

These requests don't include your name, look, rank, progress or anything else from the game. Like any connection to a website or server, they do reveal your IP address to GitHub and to our game server's hosting provider (Fly.io), which need it to deliver the response.

Online play

When you choose to play online or use a private room, the app connects to the Bump Rush game server and sends:

  • your online name: three numbers that pick words from fixed lists built into the game (for example "Zippy Comet 27"). The name you type is never sent. You can roll a new online name on the title screen.
  • your creature's look (color, hat, eyes) and rank tier (Wood to Championship),
  • the type of device (iOS, Android or web) and the game version,
  • your game controls (joystick direction and dash presses) while you play.

The server shows your online name, look, rank badge and in-game movements to the other players in the same match or private room. It keeps this only in memory while the match or room exists. It doesn't save it, write it to its logs, or share it with anyone else. The server's own logs record only counts (for example, how many players are in a match) and technical events, never names, room codes, devices or IP addresses. The only things it can send between players are game moves and four fixed emotes; there is no chat or free text.

The server's hosting provider (Fly.io, in the United States) receives your IP address to deliver network traffic and may keep it briefly in its own technical logs, as any internet service does. We don't use IP addresses to identify anyone.

Muting

After an online match you can mute another player: their name then shows as "Player" and their emotes are hidden. This is saved only on your device.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Children

Bump Rush is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. It doesn't ask for or allow typing any personal information that other players can see, and we don't store information about any player.

Your choices and requests

You can play entirely offline against bots, roll a new online name at any time, hide other players' emotes in Settings, and mute players after an online match. We don't keep any data about you on our servers, so there is no account or stored profile to access or delete: uninstalling the app removes everything it saved. If you have a question or a privacy request, email us and we'll answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
